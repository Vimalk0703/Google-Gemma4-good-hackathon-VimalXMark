## Citation format

- **Journal article:** Author AA, Author BB, Author CC, et al. Title. *Journal Name Abbrev*. Year;Volume(Issue):Pages. doi:DOI
- **Web page / report:** Author or Organization. Title. Site Name. Published/Updated date. Accessed date. URL
- **Book / chapter:** Author AA. Title. Publisher; Year.
- **Inline:** numbered superscripts, e.g. *"…reduces under-five mortality by 15%.<sup>[6](#ref-6)</sup>"*

## Verification policy

Every reference above is re-verified before any public-facing artefact ships
(README, Kaggle submission, demo videos). Verification steps:

1. Re-fetch every URL and confirm the content matches the cited claim.
2. Re-confirm peer-reviewed papers exist on PubMed Central or Cochrane and that DOIs resolve.
3. If any source has retracted, withdrawn, or moved, replace it before any public-facing artefact uses it.

If a claim cannot be re-verified to a source listed here, it does not appear in the project's public-facing materials.
